Time Off/Shift Trade Request Form

Use this form to request time off or to request a shift trade.

For PTO submissions select a date 2 weeks out in advance to avoid an occurrence.

When submitting PTO, you can submit for 8 hours or 4 hours. Shift Trades should have 24 hours' notice; if unable to give 24 hours' notice you will be able to use an emergency shift trade up to twice a month without an occurrence.

If using a voucher, select PTO option.
